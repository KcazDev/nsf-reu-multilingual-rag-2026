RESEARCH INTERPRETATION
========================================================================
BM25 Top-2 produced the highest GPT-5.4 correctness score in this 20-question pilot.
The filtered BM25 condition outperformed the all-document condition, supporting the conclusion that removing distractor evidence can improve answer generation.
The result does not prove that BM25 is superior to oracle retrieval because the sample is small and GPT-5.4 sometimes answered correctly even when BM25 did not retrieve all XRAG-labeled support documents.
